About

A bit about me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Passau, supervised by Prof. Steffen Herbold. My research focuses on understanding and shaping what language models learn. I develop GRADIEND, a gradient-based method that learns interpretable feature directions inside neural networks and uses them to detect and change model behaviour, for example to reduce social biases. I also work on mathematical NLP, where I developed MAMUT, a framework for generating specialized math datasets for training language models.

Before my PhD I studied Computer Science and Mathematics at TU Dresden. I teach AI Engineering and Deep Learning for NLP.

Interests: Language Models · Explainable AI · Mechanistic Interpretability · Bias & Fairness · Math NLP
