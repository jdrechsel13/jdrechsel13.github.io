About

About me

I am a PhD student in Computer Science at the University of Passau, supervised by Prof. Steffen Herbold. My thesis topic is targeted feature learning: finding the directions inside a language model that encode a specific feature, and using them to detect and change the model's behaviour. For this I develop GRADIEND, which learns such a feature from model gradients. I applied it to German grammar and released it as an open-source Python package.

As a side project I work on mathematical language: MAMUT generates formula variations for training math-aware language models. It grew out of my Master's thesis. Before my PhD I studied Computer Science and Mathematics at TU Dresden. I teach AI Engineering and Deep Learning for NLP.

Research interests: Targeted Feature Learning · Language Models · Explainable AI · Mechanistic Interpretability
